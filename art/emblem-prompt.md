# Azeroth Complete — addon emblem

Generate one square image, 1024 x 1024 pixels, and save it as
`art/emblem.png`, relative to the root of the repository. Do not overwrite an existing file:
if it already exists, save as `emblem-2.png`, `emblem-3.png` and so on.

## Subject

A round heraldic medallion used as the icon of a World of Warcraft addon about completing
everything in the world (quests, collections, achievements). It must read clearly when
shrunk to 20 x 20 pixels.

- A thick polished gold outer ring with a darker bronze inner bevel, slightly worn.
- Inside the ring, a deep burgundy enamel disc.
- At the center, a bold five-pointed gold star whose points reach close to the ring, with a
  small raised round boss in the middle of the star.
- Behind the star, very faint engraved lines of a compass rose (eight thin rays).
- Lighting from the top left, soft highlights on the gold, painted game-icon style
  similar in spirit to classic fantasy MMORPG icons (but an original design).

## Strict constraints

- The medallion is perfectly centered and fills about 92 % of the image width.
- The background outside the medallion is plain, flat, pure white (#FFFFFF), with no
  shadow, no glow, no texture and no other object.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no signature, no watermark anywhere.
- If the result contains any text or letters, generate the whole image again; never fix it
  with programmatic post-processing.
